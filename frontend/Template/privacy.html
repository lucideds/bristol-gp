Privacy

What data we collect

When you create an account on T Level Connect, we collect your email address and a password for signing in. These are the only two pieces of personal data our login and registration system requires.

What your data is used for

Your email address is used to identify your account and to sign you in. Your password is used only to verify it is you logging in. We do not use your email for marketing, and we do not share it with third parties.

How your password is protected

Your password is never stored as plain text. It is hashed before being saved, meaning even our own teams cannot see your actual password.

Data retention policy

We keep your account email and password for as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your email and password are permanently removed from our database within a reasonable period afterwards. We do not keep account data longer than is needed to provide the service.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to request it be corrected, and to request it be deleted. You can contact us through the contact form on the site to make any of these requests.

Cookies

If the site uses any non-essential cookies (for example analytics), you will be shown a cookie consent banner and asked to accept before they are set, in line with PECR.
