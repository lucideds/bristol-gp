Why choose a T Level?

Learn why you should consider a T Level. Knowing about them early helps you plan for your future.

  • You learn by doing

    Lessons are practical and built around careers, not just exams.

  • You get a real placement

    Spend at least 315 hours with an employer. You learn what the job is really like and build contacts.

  • It is equivalent to 3 A Levels

    A T Level is equivalent to 3 A Levels, so it is a serious qualification.

  • It keeps doors open

    After a T Level you can go to university, start an apprenticeship or go straight into work.

From Year 10 to the end of your T Level. See what happens when, how to apply for a placement, and what to do at each step.

Where are you now?

Years 10 and 11

Choose your T Level

  1. Year 10

    Explore your options

    Find out what T Levels are and how they compare with A Levels and apprenticeships. Our quiz suggests which of the five routes might suit you.

  2. Year 10 or 11

    Talk to your careers adviser

    They know which schools and colleges near you offer T Levels, and what each one asks for. Take your quiz result with you.

  3. Year 11, usually from the autumn

    Apply to a school or college

    Apply for the T Level course you want. Entry requirements vary, so check them early. Many ask for five GCSEs at grade 4 or above, including English and maths.

    Find a T Level course

  4. Summer, Year 11

    Get your GCSE results

    Your school or college confirms your place. If your results are not what you hoped for, talk to them about your options.

Years 12 and 13

Study, apply and do your placement

  1. September, Year 12

    Start your T Level

    Most of your time is spent learning at your school or college, through practical lessons and projects.

  2. Year 12

    Register your interest

    Tell us which routes you like, so the team can share information about Amazon placements. You can change your answers later.

  3. Year 12

    Apply through your school or college

    They organise placements for their students. Ask who to speak to and what they need from you, and send your application before the closing date.

  4. Year 12

    Have a short interview

    Expect questions about your interests, motivation and reasons for choosing the placement. Ask your school or college for help with preparing your CV, and whether they run a workshop where you can practise.

  5. Year 12 or 13

    Do your placement

    Spend at least 315 hours, about 45 days, with an Amazon team. It might be one block, or a day or two each week.

  6. Summer, Year 13

    Finish your T Level

    You get an overall grade: Pass, Merit, Distinction or Distinction*. You need to complete your placement to pass.

Dates vary between schools and colleges. Ask yours for the exact dates.

Common questions

What if I've never worked in a team like this?

That is normal. A placement is for learning. Ask questions and say when you are unsure.

What if I need adjustments?

Tell your school or college and your placement contact what you need before you start. They can plan support, such as changes to your hours, equipment or how tasks are explained.

What if I change my mind about the route?

Talk to your careers adviser as early as you can. You can change the routes you have registered interest in at any time. After completing your T Level, you can still explore careers outside your subject area.
