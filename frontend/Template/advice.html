Why choose a T Level

T Levels start after your GCSEs, so you don't need to decide in Year 10. But it helps to know what they are now, while you are choosing what to do next.

  • You learn by doing

    Lessons are practical and built around a career, not only exams.

  • You get a real placement

    Spend at least 315 hours with an employer. You learn what the job is really like and build contacts.

  • It counts like three A levels

    A T Level is worth the same as three A levels, so it is a serious qualification.

  • It keeps doors open

    After a T Level you can go to university, start an apprenticeship or go straight into work.

How to apply for a placement

Placements are for students on a T Level course. These are the steps, in order.

  1. Pick a T Level

    Choose a T Level course at a school or college near you. Ask your careers adviser which courses they offer.

  2. Register your interest

    Tell us which routes you like. We'll email you when applications open. You can change your answers later.

  3. Talk to your school or college

    They organise placements for their students. Ask who to speak to and what they need from you.

  4. Apply when it opens

    Applications open on set dates. Check the dates below and send your application before it closes.

  5. Prepare for the interview

    Practise answering questions about why you chose the route. Our workshop pack has a CV template and practice questions.

Common worries

What if I've never worked in a team like this?

That is normal. A placement is for learning. Ask questions and say when you are unsure.

What if I need adjustments?

Tell your school or college and your placement contact what you need before you start. They can plan support, such as changes to your hours, equipment or how tasks are explained.

What if I change my mind about the route?

Talk to your careers adviser as early as you can. You can change the routes you have registered interest in at any time.

Can my parent or carer help me prepare?

Yes. They can help you practise interview questions, check your application and plan how you will get to your placement.
