Frequently asked questions

What is a T Level?

A T Level is a two-year technical qualification for students aged 16 to 19. It combines classroom learning with an industry placement of at least 315 hours.

Who can apply for a placement?

Students who have completed their GCSEs can studying a T Level in one of the five routes: Digital, Business, Media, Finance or Engineering. Your school or college can help you apply.

Digital

T Level in Digital Production, Design and Development or Digital Support Services

What it is

The Digital pathway covers software development, IT infrastructure, and data, giving students hands on experience building and maintaining the systems a company like Amazon relies on, alongside the T Level Digital Production, Design and Development or Digital Support Services qualification.

Who it is for

Students who enjoy problem solving, logical thinking, and working with technology, even without prior coding experience, since the placement is where that skill is built.

Future job paths

  • Software developer
  • IT support technician
  • Cloud infrastructure engineer
  • Data analyst
  • Progression to a degree apprenticeship in computer science or software engineering

Business

T Level in Management and Administration

What it is

The Business pathway covers how a large organisation actually runs day to day, including operations, project coordination, and administration, paired with the T Level in Management and Administration.

Who it is for

Students who are organised, enjoy working with people, and are interested in how decisions get made and projects get delivered inside a company.

Future job paths

  • Business analyst
  • Operations coordinator
  • Project management apprentice
  • HR assistant
  • Progression into a business management degree or apprenticeship

Media

T Level in Media, Broadcast and Production

What it is

The Media pathway covers content creation, marketing, and communications, giving students experience producing the kind of material a company uses to reach customers and promote its brand, alongside the T Level in Media, Broadcast and Production.

Who it is for

Students who are creative, enjoy writing, design, video, or social media, and want a career that blends creativity with commercial purpose.

Future job paths

  • Marketing assistant
  • Content creator
  • Social media coordinator
  • Junior graphic designer
  • Progression into a marketing or media production degree or apprenticeship

Finance

T Level in Accounting

What it is

The Finance pathway covers accounting, budgeting, and financial operations, giving students real exposure to how a company tracks and manages money, paired with the T Level in Accounting.

Who it is for

Students who are detail oriented, comfortable with numbers, and interested in how businesses make financial decisions.

Future job paths

  • Accounts assistant
  • Finance apprentice
  • Payroll administrator
  • Trainee analyst
  • Progression into an accounting qualification (e.g. AAT, ACCA) or finance degree apprenticeship

Engineering

T Level in Engineering, Manufacturing, Processing and Control

What it is

The Engineering pathway covers the design, maintenance, and improvement of physical systems and equipment, such as those used in Amazon's fulfilment and logistics operations, paired with the T Level in Engineering, Manufacturing, Processing and Control.

Who it is for

Students who like working with their hands as well as their mind, enjoy practical problem solving, and are interested in how physical systems are built and kept running.
