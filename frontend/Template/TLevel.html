Frequently asked questions

What is a T Level?

T Levels are 2-year courses which are taken after GCSEs and are broadly equivalent in size to 3 A Levels. Launched in September 2020, these courses have been developed in collaboration with employers and education providers so that the content meets the needs of industry and prepares students for entry into skilled employment, an apprenticeship or related technical study through further or higher education.

T Levels offer students practical and knowledge-based learning at a school or college and on-the-job experience through an industry placement of at least 315 hours – approximately 45 days.

Who can apply for a T Level?

Students who are 16 - 19 years old when starting the course. Most providers look for a minimum of 5 GCSEs at grade 4 or above, including English and maths. Exact requirements are set individually by local schools and colleges and vary depending on the specific subject.

How do I find a work placement?

Usually your school or college finds a work placement for you. However, you can find one yourself by emailing different companies to see if they are accepting. Amazon offers work placements for five different T Level industries.

Digital

T Level in Digital Production, Design and Development or Digital Support Services

What it is

The Digital pathway covers software development, IT infrastructure, and data, giving students hands-on experience building and maintaining the systems a company like Amazon relies on, alongside the T Level Digital Production, Design and Development or Digital Support Services qualification.

Who it is for

Students who enjoy problem solving, logical thinking, and working with technology, even without prior coding experience, since the placement is where that skill is built.

Future job paths

  • Software developer
  • IT support technician
  • Cloud infrastructure engineer
  • Data analyst
  • Progression to a degree apprenticeship in computer science or software engineering

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know how to code before I start?

No. You do not need any coding experience. You learn the technical skills on your course and build on them during your placement.

What kind of work will I do on a Digital placement?

Depending on your team, you might help test software, support colleagues with IT problems, look after systems or work with data. You work on real tasks with support from the people around you.

Which Digital T Level should I choose?

Digital Production, Design and Development suits you if you want to build software. Digital Support Services suits you if you are more interested in IT support, networks or keeping systems running. Your school or college can help you decide.

Business

T Level in Management and Administration

What it is

The Business pathway covers how a large organisation actually runs day to day, including operations, project coordination, and administration, paired with the T Level in Management and Administration.

Who it is for

Students who are organised, enjoy working with people, and are interested in how decisions get made and projects get delivered inside a company.

Future job paths

  • Business analyst
  • Operations coordinator
  • Project management apprentice
  • HR assistant
  • Progression into a business management degree or apprenticeship

Frequently asked questions

What kind of work will I do on a Business placement?

You might help plan and track projects, organise meetings, keep records up to date or support a team with day-to-day operations. You see how decisions are made in a large company.

Do I need to be good at maths?

You will work with some numbers, such as budgets or schedules, but you do not need to be a maths expert. Being organised and communicating clearly matter more.

What skills will help me on a Business placement?

Being organised, managing your time, writing clearly and working well with other people all help. You build these skills further during your placement.

Media

T Level in Media, Broadcast and Production

What it is

The Media pathway covers content creation, marketing, and communications, giving students experience producing the kind of material a company uses to reach customers and promote its brand, alongside the T Level in Media, Broadcast and Production.

Who it is for

Students who are creative, enjoy writing, design, video, or social media, and want a career that blends creativity with commercial purpose.

Future job paths

  • Marketing assistant
  • Content creator
  • Social media coordinator
  • Junior graphic designer
  • Progression into a marketing or media production degree or apprenticeship

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a portfolio to apply?

No, but it helps to have examples of things you have made, such as writing, designs, videos or social media posts. School projects and hobbies count.

What kind of work will I do on a Media placement?

You might help write content, create images or video, plan social media posts or support a marketing campaign. You learn how creative work is planned and checked before it reaches customers.

Do I need my own equipment or software?

No. Any equipment or software you need for your placement work is provided.

Finance

T Level in Accounting

What it is

The Finance pathway covers accounting, budgeting, and financial operations, giving students real exposure to how a company tracks and manages money, paired with the T Level in Accounting.

Who it is for

Students who are detail-oriented, comfortable with numbers, and interested in how businesses make financial decisions.

Future job paths

  • Accounts assistant
  • Finance apprentice
  • Payroll administrator
  • Trainee analyst
  • Progression into an accounting qualification (e.g. AAT, ACCA) or finance degree apprenticeship

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to study maths at a high level?

No. You need to be comfortable working with numbers and checking details carefully. Your course teaches you the accounting skills you need.

What kind of work will I do on a Finance placement?

You might help prepare reports, check invoices and payments, update spreadsheets or support a team with budgets. You see how a large company keeps track of its money.

Can a Finance T Level lead to a professional accounting qualification?

Yes. Many students go on to study for qualifications such as AAT or ACCA, often through an apprenticeship where you earn while you learn.

Engineering

T Level in Engineering, Manufacturing, Processing and Control

What it is

The Engineering pathway covers the design, maintenance, and improvement of physical systems and equipment, such as those used in Amazon's fulfilment and logistics operations, paired with the T Level in Engineering, Manufacturing, Processing and Control.

Who it is for

Students who like working with their hands as well as their mind, enjoy practical problem solving, and are interested in how physical systems are built and kept running.

Frequently asked questions

What kind of work will I do on an Engineering placement?

You might help look after equipment, find and fix faults, or work on ways to make systems run better, such as those used in fulfilment and logistics sites.

Will I work in a warehouse or an office?

It depends on your placement. Engineering work often takes place on site, where the equipment is, with some time spent planning and recording work at a desk.

Will I need safety clothing?

On site you may need to wear safety clothing, such as safety boots or a high-visibility vest. You will be told what you need and get safety training before you start.
